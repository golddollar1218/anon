Privacy

What is private, and what is not

Cryptographic confidentiality (hiding amounts and parties inside a transaction) is not the same as network anonymity (hiding who broadcast it). Cross-chain swaps and Pump.fun launches are not anonymous.

Monero

RingCT with CLSAG ring signatures (16 members), stealth addresses and Bulletproofs+. Private by default.

Wallet integration blocked

Planned: client-side view-key scanning via a WASM wallet or your own monero-wallet-rpc. Needs a reviewed library; not built yet.

Zcash

Shielded pools (Sapling, Orchard/Halo 2) hide sender, recipient and amount from the whole pool. Transparent addresses are public.

Wallet integration blocked

Planned: lightwalletd with client-side proving (Zcash wallet SDKs). Needs a browser-ready, reviewed SDK; not built yet.
