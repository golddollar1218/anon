Required for a production chain
  1. Membership privacy: spends now hide the real input among up to 7 decoys (ring signature). A SNARK (Groth16/PLONK/Halo 2) over the whole pool would give a far larger anonymity set; that needs a circuit toolchain not available here.
  2. Consensus: BFT proof-of-stake (HotStuff/Tendermint family) with view change and slashing. Validator keys here are deterministic demo keys.
  3. Network privacy: Dandelion++ or mixnet transaction relay.
  4. Validation: independent cryptographic audit before any mainnet.
