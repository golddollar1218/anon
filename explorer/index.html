Today ANON offers weaker privacy than Zcash shielded transactions and Monero (ring size 16, years of research on decoy selection). Cryptographic confidentiality (hiding amounts and parties inside a tx) is different from network anonymity (hiding who broadcast it) — neither replaces the other.

Correlation risks
  • Deposit/withdraw amounts — public boundaries leak exact values.
  • Timing — short dwell time links entries to exits.
  • Input/output counts — unusual shapes fingerprint wallets.
  • Fees — non-uniform fees identify software.
  • Network layer — IP address at broadcast time needs Tor or a mixnet.
  • Small anonymity set — privacy depends on how many others use the pool.
