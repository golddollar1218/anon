Why XMR/ZEC quotes may be unavailable: ANON checks SideShift's live coin list first. When SideShift does not list a coin for your region/IP, or reports "method is disabled", the route is shown as unavailable with that reason — ANON has no other reviewed non-custodial provider and never invents a route.

Cross-chain swaps are not anonymous. Providers may restrict privacy coins by region. Cross-chain (XMR/ZEC) settlement stays unavailable until a reviewed non-custodial provider exists and licensing (money-transmission, KYC/AML, sanctions screening, privacy-coin restrictions per jurisdiction) is assessed. Solana swaps run on mainnet now and are signed by your own wallet.
